💖 About Us

🎵

What is Tiny Kindness Tunes?

Tiny Kindness Tunes is a children's book and music series that celebrates being kind, sharing, and making good choices — one cheerful story at a time. 🌈 Our first book, Wake Up And Smile, helps little ones start every day with a grateful heart and a joyful spirit.

💛

Perfect for Parents & Caregivers

Looking for a book that sparks real conversations about feelings and kindness? Tiny Kindness Tunes gives families a gentle, joyful way to explore empathy, gratitude, and treating others well — turning storytime into a moment that truly sticks with your child.

🏠 Great for home 🛏️ Perfect bedtime read 🗣️ Sparks conversation
✨

Built with Educators in Mind

Each book is designed to support Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) goals in PreK–2nd grade classrooms. Simple language, vivid storytelling, and relatable scenarios make Tiny Kindness Tunes a perfect fit for morning meetings, read-alouds, and character education lessons.

📚 SEL aligned 🏫 Classroom ready 👶 PreK – Grade 2
